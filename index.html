Better together

Learn the word. Then make it yours.

ShuFee Words teaches you shirib, to drink. ShuFee teaches you to say I drink, you drink, and we drink.

Both apps write Lebanese the same way, so what you learn in one looks the same in the other. In ShuFee Words, 29 verbs carry a Conjugate in ShuFee button that takes you straight to all eight people and four tenses.

Want to talk about what you do?

Start with ShuFee. Verbs are the backbone of every sentence, and ShuFee drills the forms you’ll use most.

Want to name the world around you?

Start with ShuFee Words. Greetings, family, food, numbers, and getting around, a few words a day.

Want the whole cup?

Use both. Learn new words in ShuFee Words and jump to ShuFee whenever a verb needs its full set of forms.
